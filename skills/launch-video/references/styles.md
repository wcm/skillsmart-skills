# Styles

One story, five films. Pick the style from the product and its audience, not from taste; say why in one line
when you hand over.

| Style | Looks like | Moves like | Good for | Avoid for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `clean` | soft light background with drifting colour, a white browser or phone with a deep soft shadow, Inter Tight | words rise; the device tilts up into place; screens swap with speed-matched slides; slow zooms into the focus region | SaaS, productivity, AI tools, fintech, anything "premium and calm" | loud consumer brands, games |
| `drafting` | near-black sheet with a fine grid, hairlines, corner marks, a title block, Space Grotesk + JetBrains Mono, one signal colour | text plots on left to right, selections with handles, a scan line wipes screens in, hatched figure boxes, dimension lines | dev tools, infrastructure, hardware, data, anything with measurable claims | warm, human, lifestyle products |
| `paper` | ivory paper with grain, cut-paper strips and notes with hand-cut edges, screenshots as white-bordered prints, Fraunces | stop motion on twos (12 fps), every piece "boils" a little; prints drop onto a pile; the proof is a stamped starburst | creative tools, education, community, food, small brands, anything friendly | serious enterprise, security |
| `pixel` | 8-bit night: banded sky, twinkling stars, scanlines, a retro window "APP.EXE", dithered screenshots, Press Start 2P | typewriter text with a block cursor, screens load in steps, a score that counts up, PRESS START | indie apps, games, dev side projects, playful launches | anything needing photo-real screens |
| `bold` | black with one loud accent, huge Anton capitals, thick-bordered device with a hard accent shadow | hard cuts on a beat: the hook in bursts that fill the frame, problems struck through, a full-frame flash before each feature | consumer launches, events, sports, music, anything that should feel fast | quiet premium products |

## The accent

- Every style has its own accent; `accent: "#…"` swaps in the brand colour.
- Check contrast: in `bold` the accent is also a background behind black text; in `drafting` it's thin lines on
  near-black, so it must be bright.

## Mixing

Don't mix styles in one film. For a series (several launches), keep the style and change the accent.
