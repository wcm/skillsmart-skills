# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-01
- First release: a hero you dive into level after level. The camera zooms around one fixed point; each close-up comes into focus over the exact area it came from, in its parent's tone, with no pasted edge.
- A viewfinder interface: spots with size labels, brackets that grow with a dive, live field and zoom readouts, a log scale, breadcrumbs, pinned notes, a loupe ring on the spot under your cursor. Scroll, arrow keys, Esc and number keys work too.
- Tools: pictures generated with Skillsmart credits or your own first photo (`pictures.mjs`), `takes.mjs`, `look.mjs`, `align.mjs`, `sheet.mjs`, `publish.mjs`, a headless check (`check.mjs`), screenshots (`shot.mjs`) and a dive video (`video.mjs`).
- Four example sites: Pruine, Understory, Surface Theory, Where's Pip?
