# The example sites

Four sites, built while the engine was worked out. Each folder holds the page as built: `index.html`, `style.css`, `main.js`, `tree.json` (every level's prompt, area and size) and `img/` (the 1600 px pictures and `levels.json`; the sharper 3200 px ones aren't shipped). Every brand is made up; every picture was generated (with Nano Banana 2.1 and GPT Image, while the engine was worked out; the skill's tools use Nano Banana Pro, so your takes will differ a little). The live pages are at [demos.skillsmart.io/zoom-hero](https://demos.skillsmart.io/zoom-hero/).

`new_project.py <project-dir> <id> --from <name>` starts a new page from one of them; add `--examples` to copy it as it is.

| Page | Subject | Main way down | Side ways | Type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `pruine` | Pruine, natural wine | hill → old vines → one grape's bloom → wild yeast, and a shop card | limestone → fossil shell; cellar → barrel | Instrument Serif, Instrument Sans, IBM Plex Mono |
| `understory` | Understory, a rainforest trust | canopy → mossy branch → glass frog → its eye with the forest reflected, and a donation card | sloth → algae in its fur; river → morpho wing scales | Archivo wide, DM Mono |
| `surface-theory` | Surface Theory, skincare | face → cheek → a drop of serum → skin cells → a molecule, and a product card | bottle → dropper tip | Geist, Geist Mono |
| `wheres-pip` | Where's Pip?, a picture book | a clue game: town → window → note unlocks the boat → scarf unlocks the lighthouse → Pip, and a pre-order card | | Bricolage Grotesque, Space Mono |

What each taught (it is in the skill's rules now):

- **Pruine**: a wide scene needs its subjects close and prominent; a vine in the foreground gives the dive somewhere to go.
- **Understory**: a reveal works when the crop has a surface for the subject to sit on, and a frog is worth more than any amount of moss.
- **Surface Theory**: a dive can change medium (photo, microscope, 3D render) if tone and rough shapes carry over; on a phone the title needs its own card.
- **Where's Pip?**: illustrations need wider areas than photos, or the lines come out blown up; the engine can run a game.
