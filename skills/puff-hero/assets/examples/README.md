# The reference build

Four pages, one per material, built while the method was worked out. `pages.json` here holds their prompt slots
exactly as generated (they slot into a project's `tools/pages.json`); the table in `references/page.md` gives each
page's colours, fonts, background and placement. The clips on
[skillsmart.io/skills/puff-hero](https://skillsmart.io/skills/puff-hero) show them.

| Page | Words | Material | Motion | Background | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| hug | HUG / GER | satin vinyl, hot pink | the pop (its earlier wording, motion.md) | rows of HUGGER in outline | the loop on the page squeezes in with soft wrinkles, which reads as being hugged |
| pop | POP / STAR | gloss bubblegum pool-toy vinyl | the pop (its earlier wording, motion.md) | twisted lime grid + a giant "turn it up." | the loop swells to 105% and jiggles; `negative` adds "wrinkles, crinkles, deflating, marbling, stripes" |
| soft | SOFT / SERVE | matte rubber foam, four colours | its own: a jelly on a plate | soft-serve swirl + sprinkles | the four colours are named in the material |
| cozy | HELLO / COZY / SEASON | faux fur | its own: a pillow dropped on a bed | crayon clouds | `"matte": "matting"` for the fur's edges; three lines is the most to try |

Use them to see the shape of a good entry, not to copy: a new page's material, subject, shine and motion are written
for its own words and brand.
