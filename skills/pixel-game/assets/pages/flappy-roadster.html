Four ways to play

Flappy Roadster
TAP fires the cold-gas thrusters through neon pipes. Three ⚡ trigger Ludicrous.
Kickflip Roadster
Jump rush-hour traffic. Tap to jump, tap again in the air to kickflip, hold for height. Land on your wheels.
Upside-Down Roadster
Tap to flip between floor and ceiling; the suction fans hold you on. Mind the gap.
Green Light Roadster
Tap the moment the tree turns green and beat Tesla's 0-60 of 0.81 s.
Every crash
Delays Roadster production another year. A great run pulls it back in.

For creators
