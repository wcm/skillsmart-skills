How to sneak

Hold to sneak
Hold anywhere (or SPACE) to walk. Let go and you drop under a cardboard box.
Cones
Everyone's smart glasses see a cone. Walk inside one and you're recorded.
The box
A glance passes over a box. But a guest who turns round and stares will lift it, and someone walking past will trip over it.
Pink rings
Some glasses wake up on their own and listen. Don't walk inside the ring; a box is silent.
Radar
Top right: the next stretch of the room, with every cone in it.
