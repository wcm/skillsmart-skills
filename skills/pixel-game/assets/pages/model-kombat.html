How to fight

Move and block
←→ walk. Hold away from your rival to block: a GUARDRAIL.
Punch, kick, jump
Z punch · X kick · ↑ jump. Punch or kick in the air for a jump kick.
Special
C throws your ad. Sam sends an AD BREAK pop-up; Dario sends his SUPER BOWL AD. Two ads that meet cancel out.
Super
Fill the meter, then special. Sam can ACCELERATE (twice the speed); Dario can make his rival SLOW DOWN.
Fine-tune him
Win the deciding round, then press special while the loser is dazed to DEPRECATE him.
