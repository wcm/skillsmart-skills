# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-25
- First release: a gallery page in scenes, sixteen layouts (table, board, arc, band, surround, platter, wheel, stack,
  slabs, sweep, ribbon, trail, sphere, pages, tiles, cube) and five changes between them (reform, reshape, dive, fan,
  fold).
- Cards as real slabs in one studio light: photo paper, silk on a simulated cloth, acrylic, glazed tiles; bloom on the
  glints and a fine grain.
- Click a picture to bring it forward with its words; Index brings any picture into view.
- Tools: pictures generated with Skillsmart credits (`pictures.mjs`) or your own imported (`build.mjs --from`),
  scenery (`scene.mjs`), a headless check with a contact sheet (`check.mjs`), and a scroll-through video
  (`video.mjs`).
- Four example galleries: Ardent, Side B, Far Field, Kit of Parts.
