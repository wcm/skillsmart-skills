# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-08

- First release: a gallery, then a page per subject where the subject turns on the spot as the pages snap past. Each page has its own fixed stage clipped by its shape, so the line between pages is where one subject turns into the next. Snapping turns (a trackpad's momentum never adds a second), drag to turn, flick to spin, five lines (straight, arc, slash, wave, zigzag), four galleries (row, ring, lineup, tiles), stage props that fly in, and a colour pop.
- Tools: subjects through Skillsmart credits (nano-banana-pro photos, h3-max-turbo 360° turns that start and end on the photo, birefnet-video cut-outs, sam-3-video plates under dishes, sam-3 props), one stage for all subjects, a headless check that scrolls the page like a visitor, and a looping video.
- Four example pages: In the Round, Lacquer, SIP CLUB and Torque.
