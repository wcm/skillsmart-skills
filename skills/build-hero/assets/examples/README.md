# The four example pages

Each folder has the page's code (`index.html`, `main.js`, `style.css`) and its `scene.json`: every prompt, film segment
and frame choice. The frames and pictures aren't here, because they are too heavy; see the pages live:

| Example | Business | The build | Evening | Signature | Live |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `tidelight` | a developer selling a tower before it's built | three segments: the drone sinks, tilts and swings round as floors stack; pulls back as the tower tops out | windows light floor by floor from the ground | a floor ruler that counts floors, then lights them | [demo](https://demos.skillsmart.io/build-hero/tidelight/) |
| `misura` | a kitchen maker | one take: panels on the floor rise into the kitchen as the camera comes down | LED strips and pendants, left to right | the film inside a working drawing | [demo](https://demos.skillsmart.io/build-hero/misura/) |
| `kebun` | a pool and garden builder | one take: the pool is dug, lined and filled; the garden goes in | the pool glows, then the garden, outward | glazed depth-marker tiles count days, litres and depth | [demo](https://demos.skillsmart.io/build-hero/kebun/) |
| `cerise` | a wedding and event stylist | a time-lapse in three segments: a blurred crew lay the linen, set the roses, carry in the chairs | candles first, then the room | a cherry pen line draws the table | [demo](https://demos.skillsmart.io/build-hero/cerise/) |

Tidelight and Cerise show the method this skill uses: short Kling segments between keyframes. Misura's and Kebun's films
are single takes from an earlier round on a different video model, so their scene files are a guide to the pictures and
the wording; make those builds in segments too.
