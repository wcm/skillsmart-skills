How we build · 01—06

Red earth to first swim, in six steps.

One crew does it all, from the first stake to the last fern, so nothing waits for anyone else. A pool and garden like this one takes sixty working days, about twelve weeks.

  1. 01

    Stake out

    We string the pool out on the ground and walk it with you.

  2. 02

    Dig

    A small digger spares the rain tree’s roots, then the first walls go in.

  3. 03

    Shell

    The walls, then the floor, cured for a week.

  4. 04

    Stone

    Green stone, the reason the water looks jade.

  5. 05

    Fill

    Filled in a day, then edged in pale granite.

  6. 06

    Garden

    Palms, frangipani and ferns from growers in Lim Chu Kang.

Pool and garden

Drawn together, built together.

Most pools are dropped into a lawn. Ours are drawn with the garden around them: where the rain tree throws its shade at four, where the frangipani drops its flowers, where the afternoon storm runs off. The same crew lays the stone and plants the beds, so the pool looks as if it was always there.
