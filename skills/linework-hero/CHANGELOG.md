# Changelog

## 1.1.0 — 2026-10-13
- The drawing is the hero: a new `fill` option scales a drawing and its labels to fill whatever box the page gives
  it (centred, or aligned with `fill: [x, y]`), and `crop` / `handle.crop()` choose the view, for a camera that
  pulls back as you scroll. A page's own sizing of the host now always wins over the engine's defaults.
- The four example pages rebuilt around it: each drawing as large as the screen allows, with room to breathe, and
  far fewer words. The blog post holds one diagram beside the text and builds it stop by stop; the lens page opens
  on the whole lens and pulls back as it comes apart; the shop and the tracker fill the screen beside their controls.
- Shorter callouts on the lens, so the exploded view fills the width.
- The instructions say how big to draw, where the words go, and how to hold a drawing while text scrolls past.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-12
- First release on Skillsmart (developed as Linework): draw my system (stacks, pipelines, processes and physical
  assemblies as animated isometric diagrams with page hooks), interactive figures, fourteen looks, four real example
  pages, and a checker that shoots every state and look onto one sheet.
