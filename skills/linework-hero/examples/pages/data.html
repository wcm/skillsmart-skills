How a tap becomes a chart

Two billion events a day, seven stops each. Follow one.

PRTEPriya Raman & Tom Ellery
Follow one event
Stop 1 · Sources

Every tap is an event

Apps batch them and stamp each with an ID.

2.1 B a day
Stop 2 · Collector

The collector takes them in

It checks, stamps and writes. Nothing else.

12k a second at peak
Stop 3 · Stream

The stream keeps them in order

Seven days of replay for every reader.

Stop 4 · Processors

Processors clean them up

Drop duplicates, join accounts, add places.

Stop 5 · Lake

The raw copy goes to the lake

Byte for byte, so anything can be rebuilt.

Stop 6 · Warehouse

Tables land in the warehouse

Click it to open its layers.

Stop 7 · Dashboard

And the chart moves

48 s tap to chart, p95
The cheapest copy of your data is the one that lets you be wrong.
