# From an idea to a concept

A concept is an object, what the pointer does to it, what the read-out says, and a look. It fits on one line.

## Finding it

1. **Find the object.** Something you could put on a desk, not a diagram. A deploy pipeline is not boxes and
   arrows; it might be a flight of canal locks. Search is a card catalogue. Growth is a potting bench.
2. **Find what gives it away.** For each kind of part, name the two or three features that make it that thing
   and nothing else: a pot has a rim band and a taper; a cactus has ribs and an arm; a drawer has a pull and a
   label holder; a synth has keys, a big knob and patch cables. Write the list down; the check asks you to find
   each feature in the thumbnail.
3. **Find the variable.** The one number or choice that matters: how far, which one, how fast, how open.
4. **Give it to the pointer.** Where the pointer is sets a position (continuous: springs); what it is over sets a
   choice (discrete: tweens, staggered by distance). Scroll is a third hand for something that unfolds.
5. **Design the rest.** It is the thumbnail. Make it uneven and already about the idea: the data as heights, one
   drawer ajar, a lens a little apart. Light one thing.
6. **Choose the read-out.** A few characters naming what is under the pointer (`B3`, `cutoff 1.2k`,
   `aperture f/2.8`), and `rest`.
7. **Choose the slider's number.** One number that makes the answer weaker or stronger: a reach, a spread, a
   resonance, a lift.
8. **Choose the look** from the subject (`references/looks.md`).

## Answers that work

| Answer | Example | The pointer | The clock | The slider |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Field** | greenhouse | a position on a plane that never moves; height falls off with distance | a spring per part | the reach |
| **Pick** | catalog | the thing under it (rest areas); neighbours answer less and later | tweens, delay = distance × 45ms | the spread |
| **Scrub** | synth | x is a value; everything that shows the value follows it | springs; a kick for things that swing | the strength |
| **Explode** | lens | scroll (or x) spreads parts along an axis; the pointer picks one, from the targets | springs | the spread |
| **Paint** | (Hairline's Phosphor) | excites what it passes; the marks fade | a decay per part | the afterglow |
| **Dilate** | (Hairline's Slow) | the world keeps moving; hovering slows it | a spring on the rate | the rate |
| **Spin** | (Hairline's Turntable) | flicks it round; friction and detents | friction, then a spring | the coast |

Click adds one moment to any of them (the catalog lifts a card). Keep it to one.

## An empty state

Draw the absence, not a sad face: the tool that holds things, holding none. A drawer pulled out with no cards, a
bench of empty pots, a lens with no glass. Rest does all the work; the read-out counts to zero (`B3 · 0 cards`).

## A weak concept

Drop it or fix it when it is dead at rest, needs a label to be understood, holds two ideas, gives the pointer no
reason, is a diagram, is just the icon for the idea (a padlock for security), depends on one colour, or will not
read at 240px.

## Four concepts for "a deploy pipeline"

> **Locks.** A flight of canal locks stepping down; the pointer picks a lock, its gates open and the water drops
> to the next, staggered from the pointer. `lock 3`. Blueprint: it is civil engineering.
>
> **Marble run.** A frame of three zigzag ramps with marbles always rolling; hovering slows the run so a marble
> can be followed. `ramp 2`. Neon: it is a toy at night.
>
> **Cabinet.** A server cabinet of twelve blades, a few half out where the last update stopped; the pointer's
> height sets where the update is. `blade 7`. Terminal.
>
> **Post room.** Pigeonholes; the pointer picks a slot and its letters slide out, the click sends one. `slot C2`.
> Riso: it is paper.
