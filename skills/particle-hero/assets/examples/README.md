# Examples

Four pages on the same engine, each in its own look. Every page ships with its subjects (`img/`: each cut-out and its
depth map), so `new_project.py … --from <name>` gives you a working page to change. The prompts that made the subjects
are in each folder's `pictures.json`. They were made with GPT Image 2.6 on fal.ai; the skill's own tools make subjects
with nano-banana-pro on your Skillsmart credits.

| Page | For | Look | Change | Live |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `fauna/` | *Fauna Borealis*, an exhibition at a natural history museum: reindeer, snowy owl, arctic wolf, wild horse | `dust`: bone-white dust on black, a wind taking grains off the animal's back, trackers with coordinates and a ruled frame | swarm | [fauna](https://demos.skillsmart.io/particle-hero/fauna/) |
| `herbier/` | *Herbier*, a perfume house: the rose and *Rose de Mai*, the fig and *Figue Verte* | `ink`: coloured ink on cream paper, an editorial serif | mist: the plant evaporates from the stem up, the bottle condenses out of it | [herbier](https://demos.skillsmart.io/particle-hero/herbier/) |
| `afterglow/` | *Afterglow*, a three-night festival: four artists | `neon`: neon dust, three lights circling and flashing on a 118 BPM beat, streaks, the pointer as a warm light; the giant name stands behind the dust | swarm on the downbeat, every three bars | [afterglow](https://demos.skillsmart.io/particle-hero/afterglow/) |
| `stride/` | *Stride*, a sneaker range: runner, court, trail, slip-on | `print`: fine orange dots on off-white that never pile darker than the orange, a scan line, callouts on leader lines, a huge thin model name | melt along matched grains, toe to heel | [stride](https://demos.skillsmart.io/particle-hero/stride/) |

Every brand, artist, price and date is made up.
