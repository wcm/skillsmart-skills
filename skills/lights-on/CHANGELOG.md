# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-01
- First release: one photographed place in two lights, composited as real light in WebGL2. The sun drains toward the
  window with a golden-hour grade, then each light comes on by its own curve: warm, neon, candle, LED or fade.
- Each light's share is measured from a photo of it alone. Variant sets (fabrics, materials) wipe in along a rod.
- Tools: pictures generated with Skillsmart credits as one day photo and its edits (`shots.mjs`), lined up and measured
  (`prep.py`), a headless check with a contact sheet (`check.mjs`), and a video (`video.mjs`).
- Optional room sound that follows the light and stays off until the visitor asks.
- Four example pages: Noble Gas (a switch), Lueur (click a lamp), Pli (a fabric and the time), Deux Temps (scroll).
