# Motion script

## Beat sheet (12 s)

| Beat | Time | Words | Motion |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Tease | 0.0–1.4 | small caps line ("Something new") | letter-spacing settles |
| 2 Word | 1.4–3.0 | 1 word | scale down from 240% |
| 3 Flash | 3.0–4.2 | 1 word on accent background | slide up, hard cut |
| 4 Stack | 4.2–6.8 | 3 short lines, middle in accent | wipe reveal |
| 5 Marquee | 6.8–9.4 | phrase repeated | scroll + accent line grows |
| 6 End | 9.4–12.2 | brand + date/URL | rise and hold |
