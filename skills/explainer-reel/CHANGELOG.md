# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-21

- First release: a script or a topic becomes a narrated explainer reel. One Gemini voice reads the whole script with a
  style direction, Whisper times every word (both in one Skillsmart call, paid with credits), and the pictures land on
  the words. Particle swarms that re-form from subject to subject, turning 3D forms, flow fields, engraved and dotted
  fills, eight looks, ambient sound beds with cues, and three finished examples (moon, eyes, renew). MP4 in 9:16, 4:5,
  1:1 and 16:9 from one reel, rendered frame by frame by Skillsmart's renderer.
